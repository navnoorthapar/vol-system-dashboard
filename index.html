Joint SPX/VIX calibration

An end-of-day research system for SPX and VIX options. Its daily job is built to store each U.S. session's option books, fit an arbitrage-free market surface and one stochastic-volatility model jointly to the SPX smile and the VIX index term structure (VIX options are a research leg, not in the fit), and score both on quotes they were not fitted to, in units of the quoted half-spread; the pipeline panel lists what is stored so far. Greeks, scenarios and a mark-to-model backtest with its statistical inference are built from the same fits.

Findings at a glance

✓Works

  • Arbitrage-free SVI surface: median 1.60 half-spreads from the mid, 31.0% inside the bid–ask, on the held-out strikes of 2026-10-26.
  • VIX replicated from the stored chain: −0.34 index pts from the Cboe close (replicated − official) on 2026-10-26, by the Cboe formula, with 12 listed departures (Surface).
  • The gate keeps broken-data fits off the page: 37 pre-C22 fits labelled legacy, none published; 6 current-schema fits stored (the job stores only fits that pass the gate; a rejected fit is recorded in its run log, not stored).

✕Does not work

  • One-factor Heston at the short end: 4.20 vol pts out of sample on the 2026-11-06 expiry (11 days), VVIX 198 against 101 (2026-10-26).
  • Heston's ATM skew is steeper than the market's: ×1.06 to ×1.70 the SVI-cal market skew at every listed expiry of 2026-10-26 (|skew| 1.91 against 1.14 vol per unit ln(K/F) at 11 d); the market's slope in T is −0.45 to −0.32 over 6 sessions (SVI-cal, all expiries).
  • The mark-to-model backtest loses: −22.2% over 2018–2025, Sharpe −0.99 (95% CI −1.81 to −0.30).
  • The ML regime classifier: 74.9% out-of-sample accuracy against 87.0% for the predict-yesterday baseline; research only, it never trades.

→Next · not done yet

  • Forward test at executable prices: not yet evidence: 1 registered session so far; the ledger fixes targets before any later price exists.
  • Rough Heston in production: not adopted: lower out-of-sample error on 4 of 6 sessions, but H is weakly identified (2026-10-26).
  • VX futures in the joint objective: not done: the fits score VX settlements out of sample only, 1.85–3.18 index pts RMSE on 4 of 6 gated fits, the model below the settlement on 35 of 35 contracts. VX futures table ends 2026-10-09; none stored for 2026-10-26.
  • A wider production σ box: not done: σ ≤ 2 is kept because the NN pricer's domain, the vomma shock and the bound chips are tied to it (Corrections C23-M1).

Methodology and caveats

The forecast is the expected realised vol (RMS) of the next 21 sessions, the VIX horizon. VIX is a risk-neutral variance quote, so the spread mixes the variance risk premium with a convexity gap (E[σ] < √E[σ²]); it is not a pure premium. Forecast and history are the purged walk-forward forecast (refitted daily on labels known at each close), so the percentile ranks today against out-of-sample values only.

σ₁ (EWMA, 5-day half-life) 10.97%; σ₂ (EWMA, 60-day half-life) 12.25%. Walk-forward R²: 0.29 (ex-2020 0.26) on 3,623 forecasts 2012-04-30 → 2026-09-25.

COVID stress test, 2020-03-16: forecast made at the 2020-03-13 close (refitted on the 2,484 labels known then) 54.5%. Realised over its 21-session horizon (2020-03-16 → 2020-04-14, RMS, annualised): 77.1%; error −22.5 vol pts. A backward-looking model cannot see a jump coming.
