VIX minus PDV forecast from 2026-07-31 to 2026-10-23: first +1.41 vol pts, last +5.20 vol pts, range +1.41 to +6.16 vol pts.

The forecast is the expected realised vol (RMS) of the next 21 sessions, the VIX horizon. VIX is a risk-neutral variance quote, so the spread mixes the variance risk premium with a convexity gap (E[σ] < √E[σ²]); it is not a pure premium. Forecast and history are the purged walk-forward forecast (refitted daily on labels known at each close), so the percentile ranks today against out-of-sample values only, not against zero.
