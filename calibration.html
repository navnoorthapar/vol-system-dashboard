How the skews are computed
  • SVI-cal (the Surface page’s fitted slices): the analytic slope of each fitted slice at the forward, w′(0) / (2·√(w(0)·T)), with w′(k) = b(ρ + (k − m)/√((k − m)² + σ²)).
  • eSSVI: the surface report’s stored ATM skew (term_structure.atm_skew_essvi), 2026-11-03 only. eSSVI’s ψ(θ) shapes the skew across expiries, so its short end differs from SVI-cal’s; both are shown, neither is preferred.
  • Heston: central differences of the model smile at h = 0.01 and 0.005 in ln(K/F), Richardson-extrapolated, at 2026-11-03’s listed maturities: the published parameters (κ 4.83, σ 2.00) and the σ-profile refit with σ ≤ 10 (κ 36.57, σ 10.00; calibrations/diagnostics/2026-11-03.json).
  • Skews are negative (puts over calls); the chart plots magnitudes on log axes, so a straight line is a power law.
