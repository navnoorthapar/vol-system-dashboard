Diagnostics

!
Feller condition: 2κθ < σ² — VIOLATED
2κθ = 0.49862; σ² = 4.00000; margin 2κθ − σ² = −3.5014. Diagnostic only — not imposed. 2κθ ≥ σ² is not a no-arbitrage condition: when it fails the variance process can reach zero and reflects, and the Fourier pricer remains valid. Here the SPX smile and VIX indices together ask for more vol-of-vol than the Feller boundary allows.
i
Identification — 4 of 5 effective degrees of freedom
Active constraints: sigma = 2 (box ceiling). A parameter on a bound is set by the constraint, not by the market data, so this 5-parameter model is effectively fitting with 4. Fit from 53 SPX options (6 expiries) and 4 VIX tenors, all taken on the closed SPX session of 2026-10-16. The VIX leg needs at least 3 tenors to identify a curve; a single tenor is one data point that Heston matches exactly, and the joint fit would silently collapse into an SPX-only calibration.
