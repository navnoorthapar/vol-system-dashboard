Where the replication departs from Cboe
  • parity_forward replaces the single-strike forward with the cleaned chain's parity-regression F and D (robust to one stale strike).

  • VIX9D/VIX3M/VIX6M use the VIX candidate set and bracket rule at 9/93/184 days (analogues, not Cboe's index-specific series sets).
