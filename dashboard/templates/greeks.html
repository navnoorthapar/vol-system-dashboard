QV Convexity × 10⁴ — by Maturity
Variance of the integrated variance ∫₀ᵀ v_t dt under the surface's Heston parameters (Monte Carlo), one value per maturity — it does not depend on strike. A model quantity: it grows with the horizon because more variance paths accumulate, not because of any option's vomma.
Mean Vanna — by Maturity (∂²V/∂S∂σ)
Black–Scholes vanna averaged over the strikes computed at each maturity. Vanna = −φ(d₁)·d₂/σ is negative below the forward and positive above it, so this average mostly reflects which strikes the surface holds; it is not a measure of the spot–vol leverage effect.
