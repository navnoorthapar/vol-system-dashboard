Methodology and caveats

QV convexity is the variance of integrated variance ∫₀ᵀ v_t dt under the surface's Heston parameters (exact closed form for the CIR variance); it does not depend on strike. Vanna ((∂²V/∂S∂σ)) = −φ(d₁)·d₂/σ is negative below the forward and positive above it, so its average mostly reflects which strikes the surface holds; it is not a measure of the spot–vol leverage effect.

Methodology and caveats

Each day's P&L is split into Θ, Γ, ν (realised Δσ), vanna, volga and financing from the previous close's Greeks; the residual is what the second-order expansion misses (third-order terms, and the vol path between closes).
